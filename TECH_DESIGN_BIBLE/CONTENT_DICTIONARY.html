Content Dictionary

Living registry Opened: 2026-08-23 (Enrra's order) Authority for demands: the bible + AMENDMENTS.html

The registry of approved content. Content authoring is mostly free; this dictionary is the handle on it, because content drives engineering — a card that needs a condition kind, a mutator, an event name, or a container the rosters lack is making an engineering demand. The rule: a content piece whose demands are not all ruled is not authorable; the demand surfaces for ruling first, and the ruling lands in AMENDMENTS.html. The dictionary records, per piece, exactly what it stands on — so every roster entry traces to the content that demanded it, and no mechanism enters through a card unseen.

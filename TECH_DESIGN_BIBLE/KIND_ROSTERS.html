Kind Rosters

Signed Authored: 2026-08-27 Mechanics: Core System Design · Mutation System Design

The registry of concrete kinds populating the system's extensible families. The designs define each family's mechanics — its signature, how a kind enters, the combination vocabulary — and appoint no exhaustive population; this document holds the population. A kind enters here only by Enrra's word. Machinery marks a kind constructed in code by the type machinery or the engine, absent from the authored vocabulary; every other kind is authorable.

§2 Resolver decisions

Mechanics: Core §4 (the resolver's two phases, unknowns, empty election).

KindMembersPurpose
nearest— Ranks by the symmetric distance from the holder's standing, electing the single nearest candidate. Deterministic; ties break by address in reading order. A candidate standing nowhere falls out.
random— Elects one candidate uniformly. The roll settles at engagement, from the world's seeded rng.
stat_rankedstat, rank Ranks by a stat, electing the single highest or lowest bearer. Deterministic; ties break to the earliest candidate; a candidate not bearing the stat falls out.
hand_pick— Consults the player through the world's picker seam; the pick settles at engagement. A declined pick yields an empty election — for an activated ask, the ask ends whole, unpaid.
occasions_target— The stock derivation: takes the target carried in the occasion's core as its own, kept to the narrowed field.
attack (machinery)— The main action's fixed targeting: ranks by BoardGeometry's attack-preference comparator, electing one. Ties break by row then column.
all (machinery)— Elects the narrowed field whole — the base rules' decision; a delivery walks every recipient.
holder (machinery)— Elects the plate's holder — the spell burial's resolver.
game (machinery)— Automatic targeting of the Game — the Card type's play-targeting fact.

The AutoResolver is the fallback resolver of an effect authored without a targeting block: it resolves as the target carried in context, falling back to the Game where none is found. No conditions, nothing unknown.

§3 Mutators

Mechanics: Mutation §4 (kind, unique, addressing, lifecycle).

KindAuthored formPurpose
stat_mutation{"stat", "delta"} Applies the delta to the recipient's stat.
damage{"amount"} Deals the amount to the recipient.
status{"id", "stacks"} Grants the recipient stacks of the status.
strike— Asks that the recipient be struck; the striker is the holder.
draw{"count"} Asks the deck's top cards to hand — one request per card, target the drawn card, recipient the drawing side. A shorter deck yields the draws that exist.
poison— The Poison status's periodic ask: damages the recipient per the holder's stacks, the damage carrying the poison kind. Stacks are a fact of the holding status, read at issuance.
pay (machinery, unique)— Runs once per delivery, before the walk; appoints the holder's side, whose mana pays. The engaged event's name derives from the occasion — play_engaged / ability_used, the ability's name carried forward.
placement (machinery)— The unit play's baked substantive: the play's resolver elects the slot — the destination target — and the mutator introduces the holder as cargo, container slotted_unit.
move_unit (machinery)— The Move ability's carrier — the same operation as the placement, each its own stake.
bury (machinery)— Asks the recipient's burial; its request targets the recipient.
tap (machinery, unique)— Appoints its holder; sets tapped — the main action's spend.
untap (machinery)— The untap rule's ask: clears the recipient's spent taps in one request.
ramp (machinery)— The ramp rule's ask: raises the recipient side's mana_capacity by the Game's mana_ramp, read at issuance. Capacity is uncapped.
refill (machinery)— The refill rule's ask: raises the recipient side's mana to capacity — a raise, never a drain.
shield_recovery (machinery)— The shield recovery rule's ask: raises the recipient unit's shield to its authored value — a raise, never a drain; a unit authored without shield recovers to nothing.

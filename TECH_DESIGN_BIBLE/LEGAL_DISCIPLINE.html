How signed documents change. A signed document's text is untouchable; its body changes only through the revision procedure below. The procedure applies to every document in the bible and to any other signed document of the project.

§1The revision procedure

  1. All invalidated text is scratched — struck through, never removed.
  2. Edits are written with a high-contrast highlight, for diff visibility.
  3. The affected document's sign-off is scratched and marked "Pending revision".
  4. Once the edits are approved, the diff is pushed to git.
  5. The diff is consolidated: scratched text is deleted, and the highlighted edits are promoted to regular wording.
  6. The document is signed off again.
  7. The signed document is pushed to git.

Steps 1–3 produce the revision for review; nothing past step 3 runs without Enrra's approval of the edits. Steps 4 and 7 are separate commits: git holds both the visible diff and the clean signed result.

At steps 3 and 6, index.html is updated to reflect the document's current state — in revision or signed.

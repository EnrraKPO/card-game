Development instructions · the card system

The Anatomy of Cards

Instructions — in authoring Drafted: 2026-08-19 Branch: nuke-channel Reference: MUTATION_INSTRUCTIONS.html (The Mutation of Facts III, on hold) — one system, two documents; amendments to it are planned as authoring here reaches them

The core of playable things: what a card, a spell, and a unit are, and how triggered and activated effects give them behavior. This document defines those kinds and the full road from a player's ask to a delivered play.

§1 Charter

This document settles:

  • The kinds — card, spell, unit: what each is, where each lives, and which capacities each bears.
  • The two effect natures — triggered and activated: how each attaches to its holder and how each comes to run.
  • The ask's cargo — the play road carries player-supplied information beyond the source's identity: the ability among its siblings, the appointed targets, the destination slot. This document names the carrier and the road.
  • The spell — the card that delivers without landing on the board; relocated here from The Mutation of Facts III's Open 29, whose recorded insights carry over as material.
  • The event anatomy — the shape of a happening: the core facts every event bears and the components it accretes on the road.
  • The condition — the predicate serving in effects and resolvers: its kinds, its authored form, and its reading of event components.

§2 The entity anatomy — draft

Draft — settled in discussion 2026-08-19, unruled.

The kind chain spends inheritance on card-ness. Card derives from GameEntity and embeds the machinery every playable thing shares: the pay effect and the payability query. Unit derives from Card and embeds the machinery every unit shares: the main action, the target poll, the placement reactor on play_engaged (Open 4), and the burial reactor on died. Spell derives from Card beside Unit and embeds the spell's machinery: the burial reactor on play_engaged (Open 4). A kind with embedded machinery is what a type expresses; the type is the home of the appointment slots.

Slot is a GameEntity. Its coordinate is a birth fact — the slot's identity on the board. The slot bears a named container, slotted unit, and board occupancy is membership in it: a unit housed in a slot's slotted-unit container is fielded there. Placement legality lives where every invariant lives — in the membership primitives all writes pass through.

The container is an object, one class for every use. Its identity: the owner entity and a name; its substance: ordered Array[GameEntity] members. It admits any GameEntity; membership legality is the writing procedure's law. Any entity organizes its containment into named containers as its anatomy warrants — a side names deck, hand, and graveyard; a slot names slotted unit.

Every housed entity bears a back-reference to its container object. The owner is one hop away through the container's owner member; the entity thereby knows both its holder and which of the holder's containers houses it. The membership primitives — the only writers of members — maintain the back-reference with every insert and remove. Fielded reads as: my housing is a slot's slotted-unit container. A unit's board position reads as: my housing's owner's coordinate.

The one-housing law: an entity is housed in exactly one container object, or unhoused at mint. A move is remove-from-housing plus insert-at-destination, one procedure's act.

Occupancy is a fact of content. A slot's slotted-unit container holds what play puts there; current content fields one unit per slot, and the mechanics carry any count.

Any GameEntity can be a target. Target eligibility spans the entity root; narrowing is the work of each resolver's authored conditions.

Geometry is a read layer. Distance, area, adjacency, and row-and-column reasoning compute from slot coordinates and occupancy, through the same reads as everything else.

Implementation note: back-references follow the established weak-reference discipline, and world copies remap both ends of every housing relation.

§3 The target resolver — draft

Draft — settled in discussion 2026-08-19, unruled.

The resolver elects the recipients of the payload. That election is its one office in the triad. It is fed the plate — the same facts a mutator receives, because both are unbounded in how they use the information: the world, the holder, the occasion, the recipient slots where the parties exist the occasion and the holder (§7), with each candidate supplied as the typed subject and the world reached through the holder. Through the occasion and the world it can resolve into anything.

Its anatomy is ordered: conditions, then decision. Conditions narrow the world to eligible candidates; the decision picks among the survivors. The decision mechanisms are a family — nearest, random, stat-ranked, hand-pick, the occasion's targets — and hand-picking is one mechanism among them: a manual resolver is a resolver whose decision consults the player.

The election runs in two phases. Resolve is the side-effect-free phase: conditions applied, the field narrowed as far as determinism reaches. It commits nothing, so any reader may run it at any moment — the interactive-idle target poll, the preview world, the greyout all read resolve. Engage is the committing phase: unknowns settle — the roll rolls, the picker picks — once, in the live flow. Engagement is issued uniformly for every resolver, and engaging is the hinge that fires the escalation into payload delivery; a resolver with nothing unknown answers its engagement from its resolution. Rolls made at engagement draw from the world's seeded rng.

An engagement can be declined. A declined pick yields an empty election: the effect is targetless, it does not trigger, and the payload never runs. For an activated ask this ends the ask whole, with everything unpaid.

An effect authored without a resolver targets the Game. The default is a fact of the authored form, decided at birth. An authored resolver is a commitment: it names the shape of its candidates and either elects them or elects nobody — and an empty election ends the delivery.

Events carry their targets as a set. The context bundle's target slot holds entities, plural; a single target is a set of one. An effect's elected targets travel in the events its delivery produces — stamped as EntityEventData under the role targets, a singular target riding as a set of one (§6) — and downstream resolvers read them from the occasion. Derivations are authored as conditions — "adjacent to the occasion's targets" is a resolver reading the occasion and narrowing by geometry.

The unique mutator. A mutator kind may bear the unique quality: the conductor runs it once per delivery, first, before the per-recipient walk, handing it the plate with the recipient slot null with a null recipient (§7). A unique mutator appoints its own target internally, from the plate. The quality is declared by the kind; the conductor reads the declaration and the seal stays shut.

§4 The play road — draft

Draft — settled in discussion 2026-08-19, unruled.

Every card bears the play effect, machinery of the Card type. Content authors its targeting; the effect itself is baked. Its anatomy:

  • Trigger: activation_invoked play, addressed to the card; the played card is the event's source (§6).
  • Conditions: affordability — the cost stat within open mana — and the holder being in hand; authored conditions join them.
  • Targeting: authored (resolver and conditions). The defaults are type facts: a card assumes auto/Game; a unit assumes manual/Slot. The engagement is where the player's pick happens; a declined pick ends the play unpaid.
  • Payload: the pay mutator, a unique mutator — it runs once per play, ahead of the walk, and addresses the holder's side, whose mana pays.

Cost is a card stat — seeded from the authored form at construction, mutable through the road like any stat. Discounts and surcharges are ordinary stat mutations.

The payment produces cost_paid play_engaged, carrying the play's elected targets as the occasion's target set as its EntityEventData under the role targets (§6). The card's substantive effects trigger on cost_paid play_engaged under the payment-is-mine source condition (§6, occasion.source == holder), and their resolvers read the occasion: the stock form takes the occasion's targets as its own; authored forms derive from them by condition.

§5 The ability — draft

Draft — settled in discussion 2026-08-20, unruled.

An ability is its holder's capability to fire an event at will — machinery any GameEntity can bear. That capability is the whole of the machinery's contribution; everything downstream of the event is authored content. The bundle that dresses the capability into a usable ability is an amenity over it.

Ask events are imperative verbs. The event vocabulary splits by direction: an ask going in is an imperative — play, use_heal — and a happening coming out is past tense — play_engaged, use_heal_engaged, died. The grammar names the side of the road a name sits on.

Every ability is a separate identity, and the identity lives in its event names. An ability named Heal owns the ask use_heal and the engagement use_heal_engaged; both derive from its name. Firing the ask names the ability whole — the ask carries no further cargo for it. The name is the event core's name fact (§6); the ask rides the core alone, componentless.

The bundle. Appointing the ability Heal on an entity expands, at construction, into three pieces:

  • The ask capability: the holder can have use_heal fired at it at will.
  • The use effect, built on the play effect's anatomy: trigger use_heal; conditions — affordability and the authored gating; targeting authored; payload — the pay mutator. Its payment produces use_heal_engaged, carrying the elected targets as the occasion's target set as its EntityEventData under the role targets (§6).
  • The substantive effect — authored — triggering on use_heal_engaged under the payment-is-mine source condition (§6).

The two discriminators bind by construction. The event name identifies the ability's kind; the payment-is-mine source condition (§6) binds each instance to its own holder's engagement. Two units bearing Heal share the event names and are told apart by the condition. Both bindings are the bundle's work; the author wires nothing.

The engaged name derives from the occasion, uniformly. The plate's occasion holds the event that fired the effect; the pay mutator reads the ask's name there and derives the produced event's name from it — play into play_engaged, use_heal into use_heal_engaged. One derivation serves both roads.

Abilities are authored through a markup form, the only authoring door. The form requests the authored fields alone: the ability's name, its cost, its targeting policy, the substantive effect. The expansion into the pieces — the ask event, the use effect, the name derivations, the discriminating conditions — is programmatic baking at construction, concealed below the form. The triggered-effect ground the mechanism extends stays machinery's vocabulary; the markup requests only what varies. The play road holds the same stance: the play effect is baked, and content authors only its fields (§4).

The play and the use are similar mechanisms, not the same one. The play effect is machinery of the Card type, one per card, asked by play; a use effect is minted per appointed ability, asked by the ability's own name. Both share the one anatomy.

§6 The event anatomy — draft

Draft — settled in discussion 2026-08-21, unruled; extended 2026-08-22 — the entity-bearing signature (with §7's routing); amended 2026-08-23 — entity-bearing facts ride one class, EntityEventData with a role member.

An event is a core plus components. The core holds the two facts inherent to every happening: the source — the entity the happening comes from, and any GameEntity can be a source — and the event's name. Every further fact rides in the event's component list: Array[EventData].

EventData is a typed class family, one class per kind shape of fact. Each component carries its fact as typed members — TargetsEventData the elected targets, StatMutationEventData the stat and the delta, MutatorEventData the manner of a mutation: the mutator's kind. A fact of an existing shape with a different purpose is not a new class — the container's ruling holds here (§2, one class for every use): purpose is a name. A fact's carrier class is the same on every event that carries the fact; the class family is the vocabulary of event facts.

Entity-bearing components share one signature. Entity-bearing facts ride one class: EntityEventData. Its members: the role — a StringName naming what the entities are to the happening, targets among them — and the entities, Array[GameEntity], a singular fact riding as a set of one. Every EventData kind whose fact holds entities yields them through the same signature: a set, Array[GameEntity], a singular fact riding as a set of one. The shared signature lets a reader gather entities across kinds without knowing any concrete kind — the gate's bare event-data route (§7) reads through it. The gate's bare event-data route (§7) pools every EntityEventData's yield; narrowed, it narrows by role.

The event accretes its components as it travels. Each layer of the road appends the components it knows: an event passing through a mutator gains its MutatorEventData; a delivery that elected targets stamps its TargetsEventData them as EntityEventData under the role targets; a procedure stamps the facts of its happening. Appending is open to anything on the road, and each layer's stamp is its own contribution.

Reading is fetch-by-shape. A reader names a component class and receives the matching components, reading their typed members. An event may carry any count of components of one shape; the fetch serves any or all, and the reader governs its use of several.

A condition asking of an absent component evaluates false. Provenance interrogation keeps its two questions over this shape: the source answers who, the MutatorEventData answers how.

The trigger names the event. An effect's trigger names its event, and the naming is itself a condition of the effect; the effect's further conditions read the named occasion's components from there.

The activation road's source. The ask event's source is the activated entity: use_heal fires with the asked unit as its source, play with the played card. The engaged event's source is the same entity, stamped by the procedure from the request. The discriminating condition queries the source — occasion.source == holder — is is_holder under the source route (§7) — one predicate, both stages, every ability and every play.

§7 The condition — draft

Draft — opened 2026-08-21; extended same day with the discussion's rulings, unruled as document text; extended 2026-08-22 — the negation handle and has_status; extended 2026-08-22 — the gate's subject routing (Open 13); amended 2026-08-23 — the event-data route narrows by role, following §6's EntityEventData ruling. Remainder: Open 8 (10 closed as duplicate; 12, 13 settled in Opens).

A condition is a predicate. It asks one question of the facts before it and answers true or false. It decides nothing beyond its answer and writes nothing.

The condition serves in two seats. In an effect, conditions gate the firing: the trigger's naming of the event is itself a condition (§6), and the further conditions join it — every one must hold for the effect to run. In a resolver, conditions narrow the world to the eligible candidates before the decision picks (§3). One concept serves both seats.

Conditions read event components by shape. A condition interrogating the occasion names a component class and reads the matching components' typed members (§6). A condition asking of an absent component evaluates false.

The rostered members so far. Two authored kinds are established. The source predicate — occasion.source == holder — the discriminating condition of every play and every ability (§6). is_holder, an EntityCondition with no members: the subject is the plate's holder. At the gate, under the source route, it is the discriminating condition of every play and every ability (§6); the source fetch is the route's work, never the predicate's. has_status, an EntityCondition with one member, status_id: StringName: the subject's contained holds a Status of that id, read by checked downcast (III §9). The machinery's baked conditions — affordability and holder-in-hand on the play effect (§4), affordability on the use effect (§5) — are conditions of those effects' anatomy, not authored content.

Derivations are conditions. A targeting rule that derives from the occasion — "adjacent to the occasion's targets" — is a resolver condition reading the occasion's target set and narrowing by geometry (§3).

The working material is the plate plus a typed subject. The plate is two facts: the occasion (an Event) and the holder (a GameEntity). The world is reached through the holder — every entity bears a back-reference to the world it lives in (III). The subject — the object the predicate runs on — is a separate input, typed, supplied by the seat: the resolver hands each candidate; the gate hands the occasion the gate hands EventConditions the occasion and EntityConditions their routed yield (routing below). The same plate serves mutators, whose recipient is likewise a separate typed input.

The family is the subject's type. A condition's base class declares what it tests and thereby its signature: EntityCondition tests a GameEntity, EventCondition tests an Event; concrete kinds derive within their base. A seat supplies one subject type and accepts that family: resolver conditions are EntityConditions; gating conditions are EventConditions, and the source predicate and the trigger's naming are their founding members the gate runs both families — EventConditions on the occasion, EntityConditions on routed subjects; the trigger's naming is the EventCondition family's founding member.

The gate routes its entity subjects. An EntityCondition serving at the gate names its subject's origin — the route, authored on the entry beside the conditions. Four routes exist: source — the occasion's core; holder — the plate's; event data — bare, pooling every entity-bearing component's EntityEventData's yield, or narrowed by a component kind id to one shape by role (§6, 2026-08-23); and world — every entity that exists, reached through the holder's world. The route list is closed: the world is the top scope, and any narrower fetch is the world narrowed by conditions. Every route yields a set of entities: source and holder as sets of one, a named component named role absent from the occasion as the empty set — genuine absence (§6).

The effect fetches; the condition only answers. Routing exists because an entity subject has a choice of origins, and the choice is authored outside the predicate. The effect owns the fetch: its gate walk reads each entry's route, gathers the yield, and hands each entity to the conditions. EventConditions carry no route — the occasion has one home, the plate — and read their components by shape themselves (§6). The rule: the effect supplies what the condition cannot locate alone.

Two lists, one gate. The effect holds its gating conditions in two typed lists, sorted at parse by family — EventConditions and entity entries. The gate holds when both lists hold; an empty list holds vacuously. Each list bears its own all-or-any policy across its entries.

The entity entry: any-fold under a negate. An entry is one route, a list of conditions conjoined per entity, and the entry's negate. The fold is any alone: the entry asks whether any entity of the yield satisfies every condition, and the negate — a handle over the folded answer — turns the existential claim universal. "All enemies are wounded" is authored "not: any (enemy, healthy)". The per-entity conjunction narrows and tests in one motion; no narrowing role and no all-fold exist at the entry. The base negate (below) keeps its seat — it inverts one condition's answer per entity; the entry's negate inverts the entry's.

The authored form of the gate. An effect's gating block holds the two lists under one key per family; each list bears its policy; an entity entry bears its route, its negate, and its conditions:

"gate": {
  "event":  { "policy": "all", "conditions": [
    { "kind": "mutator_is", "mutator": "poison" }
  ]},
  "entity": { "policy": "all", "entries": [
    { "route": "source", "conditions": [ { "kind": "is_holder" } ] },
    { "route": "world", "negate": true, "conditions": [
      { "kind": "is_enemy" },
      { "kind": "stat_vs_stat", "left": "health", "op": "lt", "right": "max_health", "negate": true }
    ]}
  ]}
}

The route token is source, holder, world, eventdata for the pooled yield, or a component kind id for one shape a role for that role's components (§6, 2026-08-23). Absent members take their defaults: policy all, negate false, an absent list empty — holding vacuously. The generic driver (below) parses the whole block; kinds shown beyond the roster are illustrative.

The families share the authored skeleton, never the call. One base class carries what every family authors alike — the kind id, the negate handle, the declared typed members and their parse, the stateless-immutable lifecycle — so the driver and the kind table stay single. The evaluation signature lives per family; no shared evaluate exists — a loosely typed subject is exactly the input this design refuses.

The family roster is open by subject type. A subject neither entity nor event founds a new family with its own routes — the container is the standing case: an origin or destination housing is a container, not a GameEntity. Membership grows under Open 8.

The predicate is the class; the handles are its members. Content never authors logic — it selects a kind by its id and fills the kind's declared typed members. A stat comparison is one kind with three members:

{ "kind": "stat_compare", "stat": "strength", "op": "gt", "value": 5 }

A check no existing kind supports is a new class, extending the family its subject dictates, plus one row in the kind table.

The collection combines; the condition never does. Each seat's condition list bears a policy — all or any — and that is the whole combination vocabulary and the combination vocabulary is the policies, the two negates, and the entity entry's any-fold (above); a condition holding conditions never exists.

Negation is a base handle. Every condition bears negate: bool, a member of the base class, false unless authored. The kind's class computes its question in its natural sense; the machinery inverts the answer where the handle is set. The driver parses the handle as base vocabulary beside the kind id. Over an absent component the inverted answer is true — absence is a genuine fact of the happening (§6), so the negated question truthfully holds.

Lifecycle: stateless immutable instances. A condition is constructed at parse, holds only its authored members, and never accumulates state across evaluations; one instance is shared safely across card copies and simulated worlds. Anything that needs memory is a world fact.

Parse is one generic driver. The kind id resolves to the class through a static table; the driver fills the class's declared members by name from the markup, refusing strangers and type mismatches loudly; serialization is the mirror walk. No kind carries parse code; the driver grows only when the value-type vocabulary grows. Authoring a kind is the class and its table row, nothing further.

Text survives parse nowhere. An authored member parses into its real type — a stat's name becomes the stat's identity, or refuses loudly because no such stat exists. The conversion is the validation; no checking layer follows it.

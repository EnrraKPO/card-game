2026-08-24 — Recovery evaluation: the slot surface

First atom: what it takes to bring back slot_ui.gd (and its neighbour board_slot.gd).

Alive and waiting. Every widget-side dependency of SlotUI survived the swap untouched: CardUI, MoveButton, BoardGlyphs, GroundPalette, HighlightFx, DragGhost, the Vfx autoload, and Interaction — the latter with the exact surface SlotUI speaks to (Role enum, role_of, commit_press), so the drop gate, drop commit, and cue/verdict agreement work unchanged.

The four dead seams in slot_ui.gd (its 5 demoted references):

  • location: BoardLocation → the core's address form, Vector3i(side,row,col) (BoardGeometry).
  • The old BoardSlot ground entity → the core's Slot (BoardManager births one per cell and indexes by address — a direct replacement).
  • BoardData.SLOT_GAP (layout constant, died with board_data.gd) → needs a new home in the recovered board layer.
  • render_ground() iterated StatusInstances on the slot → maps to core Status entities riding the Slot's containment; member names differ and must be re-verified at recovery. No slice content puts statuses on ground yet, so this half renders nothing until such content exists.

Also needed: status_pip.gd + its scene (deleted; the ground tabs preload it) — itself a cheap-band salvage with the same Status-member seam.

Not salvaged: board_slot.gd — it was engine (the ground-layer entity), and the core's Slot is its replacement.

The real cost driver: SlotUI has nowhere to stand. fight_screen.gd has no board grid — the old mounter was combat_board.gd (expensive band). The atom therefore needs a minimal grid host: a thin container that births one SlotUI per core Slot address and installs the interaction/lookup wiring. The seam edits inside SlotUI itself are small — tens of lines against its 923.

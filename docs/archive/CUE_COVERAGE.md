# Cue Coverage — the production task list

Generated by the D6 reconciliation pass (2026-07-14). **Invariant: every ENABLED entry is an
observable in-game cue with a real call site; every known cue has an entry.** Parked entries
(`enabled: false`) are authored backlog with no live site yet — never deleted, unparked when
their system lands. Produce an asset for any enabled entry from its `prompt`, drop it in
(sounds: `assets/sound/` + `file`; VFX: future asset renderer), and it is live immediately.

Resolution families (no per-id call sites by design):
- `projectile_*` / `impact_*` (27 each): resolved by `Vfx.resolve` from the source card's
  composition — `scripts/vfx/vfx_player.gd` `play()` (projectile flight + in-place impacts)
  and `scripts/combat.gd` ranged bolts. Observe: field a unit of that element; its triggered
  damage flies/lands in the element's skin.
- `combine_*` (27): resolved from the RESULT card's composition — Forge fusion flash
  (`scripts/combination_screen.gd` `_begin_fusion`) and Lab card/king forges
  (`scripts/lab_screen.gd` `_on_mint`/`_on_forge`).
- `death_*`: picked by `_death_variant` (`scripts/vfx/vfx_player.gd`) from is_king / tribe / cost.
- `aura_<status>`: attached by `_sync_status_auras` (`scripts/card_ui.gd`) while the status is held.
- Companion sounds: an entry's `sfx` field fires atomically with the visual (`Vfx.play`).
